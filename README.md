## The Promise

<table>
<tr>
<td width="52%" valign="top">

<sub><b>THE PROMISE</b></sub>

### **Brainstorm. Sharpen. Create.**

**Idea Man** is a free creative-director plugin for Claude and Codex. It finds the insight first, then generates far past the obvious with methods drawn from real master thinkers. A blind critic scores every idea, and you only see the ones that score **7+**, with their risks and the cheapest test.

Bring a half-formed thought, a rough draft, or a direction you already love. Idea Man helps you find the next useful step—and carry the idea further.

<br>

**Site:** [**sergey9519546.github.io/idea-man ↗**](https://sergey9519546.github.io/idea-man/)

</td>
<td width="48%" valign="top">

<sub><b>CREATIVE STUDIO JOURNEY</b></sub>

### **BIG IDEA. NEXT MOVE.**

![](https://img.shields.io/badge/01-Understand-F6F43C?style=flat-square&labelColor=25134D) **`→`** ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/02-Explore-26D9E6?style=flat-square&labelColor=25134D) **`→`** ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/03-Critique_(7%2B)-FF5878?style=flat-square&labelColor=25134D) **`→`** ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/04-Develop-4B39F2?style=flat-square&labelColor=25134D) **`→`** ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/05-Make-F6F43C?style=flat-square&labelColor=25134D)

1. **Understand** — Diagnose the brief and find the non-obvious insight first.
2. **Explore** — Generate far past the obvious using methods from real master thinkers.
3. **Critique** — A blind critic scores every idea cold; only **7+** ideas survive.
4. **Develop** — Surface the core risks and sharpen the strongest direction.
5. **Make** — Ship with the cheapest test, production briefs, and workspace execution.

<sub><i>A complete creative studio, from first directions to production briefs and execution with the tools available in your workspace. Save project decisions when you want to pick up later.</i></sub>

</td>
</tr>

## 05 / Character System

**Same mind. Two ways to show up.** Both characters are approved peers. Pick the silhouette that best fits the composition; their roles are flexible. Use either character—keep the attitude, the palette, and the glasses.

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

![](https://img.shields.io/badge/01-INVISIBLE_NERD-26D9E6?style=flat-square&labelColor=25134D) &nbsp; ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/APPROVED_CHARACTER-F6F43C?style=flat-square&labelColor=25134D)

### **Let curiosity lead.**
*Curiosity, with a cape.*

The empty glasses make room for the person with the idea. A compact, unusual silhouette for discovery, conversation, and the plugin icon.

* Round cyan glasses with violet depth; empty lenses.
* Coral cape, violet lining, and cyan clasp.
* No head, face, body, hands, or feet.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

![](https://img.shields.io/badge/02-LIGHTBULB_HERO-FF5878?style=flat-square&labelColor=25134D) &nbsp; ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/APPROVED_CHARACTER-F6F43C?style=flat-square&labelColor=25134D)

### **Give the idea presence.**
*A bright thought, ready to move.*

The confident pose makes a natural companion for demonstrations, studio guides, and moments when a direction starts taking shape.

* Plain cyan-blue bulb and round violet glasses.
* Coral cape, violet screw base, hands on hips, and boots.
* No eyes, eyebrows, mouth, filament, or light rays.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**Give the silhouette space.**
Use the supplied full-square artwork. Keep both lenses and the cape visible. Avoid stretching, extra props, or placing copy over the character.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**Make one character the focal point.**
Show the duo in brand introductions. For a small listing icon, choose one. These mascots identify Idea Man; the user's project keeps its own identity.

</td>
</tr>

<details>
<summary><b>📐 Typography &amp; Contrast Rules (BIG IDEA. NEXT MOVE.)</b></summary>
<br>

* **Contrast:** Use deep violet (`#25134D`) for text on yellow, cyan, coral, and light neutral surfaces (`#FFFEF4`). Use white on electric (`#4B39F2`) or deep violet (`#25134D`). Keep electric violet and coral away from small text on each other. The artwork contains its own luminous yellow and shading—keep the supplied pixels intact.
* **Headlines:** `Arial Black`, with `Segoe UI Black` and a heavy sans-serif fallback (`Display 56–120 px`, `Section 32–64 px`). Short phrases, tight tracking, generous surrounding space.
* **Reading:** `Segoe UI`, then `Arial` or a system sans-serif (`Body 16–20 px`). Sentence case, comfortable line spacing, and short paragraphs.
* **Brand name:** **IDEA MAN** for the display wordmark; **Idea Man** in prose. Keep **“Brainstorm. Sharpen. Create.”** as the product subtitle.

</details>
