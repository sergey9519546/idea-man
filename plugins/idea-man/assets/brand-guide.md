# Idea Man approved character system

Tagline: Every big idea needs a sidekick. (The capes and glasses are the sidekick.) Product line for listings: Insight first. Only ideas worth making. "Brainstorm. Sharpen. Create." is retired.

Approval: the owner approved both characters in the supplied comparison and allowed
their use in this project. Authoritative source: approved-characters.png.

## Invisible Nerd

Floating round cyan glasses, deep-violet depth/inner rims, empty lenses, coral cape
with violet lining and cyan clasp. No visible head, eyes, face or body.
invisible-nerd.png is the square listing adaptation.

## Faceless Lightbulb Hero

Plain cyan-blue bulb, round violet glasses, clear lenses showing blue bulb, violet
screw-base torso, hands on hips, short legs and boots, coral cape with violet lining,
cyan clasp. No eyes, eyebrows, mouth, yellow filament or light rays.
lightbulb-hero.png is the square alternate adaptation.

Both belong to the same brand; either may be used where suitable. Their distinctive
features remain locked during ordinary adaptations. Do not revive discarded paper
mascots or reintroduce removed facial features without a new user direction.

Palette: solar yellow #F6F43C, cyan #26D9E6, coral #FF5878,
electric violet #4B39F2, deep violet #25134D.

The bright yellow square background is intentional. Square assets were isolated with
the image-generation tool from the approved board and visually checked; the board remains
the authoritative reference. icon.png is the glasses-mark icon (512px, mark on yellow tile) used for the plugin logo and composer icon. The full illustrations are for large placements only.

Use this identity for Idea Man's own listing, guides and presentations. Do not inject
these characters, colors or style into unrelated user deliverables.
