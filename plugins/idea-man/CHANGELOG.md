  sources, approvals, requested count/format and current tools.

  blockers, stale-output invalidation and completion evidence.

  reasons, explicit superseding, version checks and atomic writes.

  mechanics; live natural-language recognition in a fresh host remains unverified.

  to Invisible Nerd. Preserved palette and existing starter prompts.

  memory, and a prompt/plan is not proof of a generated or tested artifact.
