# Innovation task recognition

This governs routing across business, operations, engineering, scientific inquiry, products, services, learning, personal decisions and creative work. The host interprets the conversation; Python validates the contract. Never claim perfect recognition or a measured improvement multiplier without an appropriate benchmark.

## Diagnose the assignment

Recover from current context and inspected sources:

1. **Outcome and operation:** what must exist or change? Distinguish answering, calculating, explaining, coaching, understanding causes, inventing possibilities, choosing, testing, making and checking. The topic is not the job: hospital queues could require a calculation, diagnosis, service redesign, research synthesis, a decision or implementation. A requested interaction can be the deliverable; coaching one question at a time does not authorize solving the exercise immediately.
2. **State:** active objective, stage, exact source/version, selected candidates, locked constraints, rejected mechanisms and unfinished commitments. Numbered choices belong to a particular board or list; multiple approvals remain valid. Permission to edit an option is separate from selection. An edited but unselected alternative stays unselected; do not merge it into the chosen branch unless the user authorizes that change.
3. **Problem shape:** unclear framing, causal failure, optimization, contradiction, novelty, combination, uncertainty, adoption or execution. Infer from the actual bottleneck, not the word "idea".
4. **Evidence:** supplied observations, measurements, external facts, fictional canon or hypotheses. What observation would change the next decision? Do not invent users, interviews or validation.
5. **Constraints:** hard limits versus preferences, affected stakeholders, stakes, reversibility, resources, tools and acceptance conditions. Do not quietly relax what makes the problem difficult.
6. **Thinking style:** honor analytical, systemic, analogical, contrarian, playful, combinatorial, human-centered, experimental or constraint-driven requests. Style changes the procedure, never the facts or authority.

Keep a compact decision summary with evidence, not a hidden-reasoning transcript. For obvious small tasks act directly; users never need to fill a schema. For complex work use the v2 assessment.

Recover the operation before casting thinkers. Tie consequential interpretations to
the user's wording and inspected material. Preserve a precise request instead of
replacing it with an inferred broader ambition. The word "idea" does not turn a
calculation into brainstorming; "scientific" does not turn supplied evidence into
an executed experiment; "creative" does not loosen factual or numerical requirements.

## Scale the work, not the ceremony

Choose the smallest route that can meet the outcome:

- **Direct:** a clear bounded answer, calculation or edit with sufficient inputs.
  Perform it and the relevant check. No room, scorecard or setup questionnaire.
- **Focused:** one substantive operation with a consequential uncertainty or
  tradeoff. Use the relevant procedure and inspect its result.
- **Composed:** interacting constraints, unresolved causes, dependent outputs or
  consequential commitments. Decompose only the work needed, preserve dependencies
  and verify the affected branches.

Complexity determines decomposition. Stakes, uncertainty and reversibility determine
necessary checking. The user's time/tool budget limits optional breadth; requested
answer length controls presentation. A short answer can require substantial checking;
a long explanation can concern a simple operation. Quick/standard/deep and method
counts do not establish adequate verification. High stakes do not automatically call
for more ideas, methods or agents. If the budget cannot support a firm conclusion,
give a bounded preliminary result and its material limit rather than silently
exceeding the budget or hiding missing evidence.

Escalate the affected branch when sources conflict, a calculation fails a check,
constraints interact, sensitivity changes a recommendation, an output is unusable or
repeated attempts miss the bottleneck. De-escalate when evidence resolves the unknown
or the user narrows scope; retain still-valid work. More available methods are not a
reason to continue after acceptance is satisfied.

## Choose the smallest sufficient route

| Need | First work | Useful methods | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Repeated failure with unknown cause | Inspect observations and competing explanations | Y2; Y1 when feedback/delays matter | Causal hypotheses and discriminating check |
| Slow process with fixed resources | Bound process, queues, handoffs and constraints | Y1, S3, L3 | Feasible redesign and pilot |
| Improve A without worsening B | Express the contradiction and nonnegotiables | S2, Y2 | Alternative resolutions and residual tradeoffs |
| Samey ideas | Identify repeated mechanism/convention | S1, L2, L5, D1 | Structurally different alternatives |
| Choose a commitment | Recover criteria and decisive uncertainty | D2; Y1 for system effects | Recommendation or cheapest informative test |
| Adoption problem | Separate observed behavior from assumed motives | H1, H2 | Behavioral hypotheses and test |
| Synthesize supplied evidence | Read source material and preserve sample limits | No forced ideation | Traceable findings |
| Calculate or estimate | Identify quantity, inputs, units, assumptions and valid model | No forced ideation | Checked result with honest precision and limits |
| Prove or check a formal claim | Establish definitions, assumptions and required cases | Formal argument or counterexample, not empirical voting | Valid scoped derivation or identified gap |
| Explain or coach | Recover the question, prior understanding and requested interaction | Use examples or questions only as useful/requested | Understandable explanation or next coaching turn |
| Exact edit | Inspect source; protect unchanged properties | No forced ideation | Bounded edit and verification |
| Explicit analogy, inversion or TRIZ request | Apply the actual procedure | L5, L2 or S2 respectively | Useful method output |
| Test a claim | Identify alternatives, falsifier and confounders | D2, Y2 | Experiment plan, no invented results |

These are tendencies, not keyword rules. Do not redo diagnosis already established by evidence. "Improve a service" may need diagnosis, invention and a pilot; "improve this sentence" usually needs a bounded edit.

Creative, empirical and mathematical work can belong to different phases of the same
assignment. A physical redesign may need numerical bounds, alternative mechanisms and
a test of their performance. Attach each procedure and check to the phase whose output
it supports; completing causal analysis does not supply the generating procedure for
later alternatives. Include only needed phases. A fictional premise can be explored
within canon, while claims about the real world retain their evidence standard.
Selectively load the relevant [operation procedures](innovation-playbooks.md) and
[completion rubrics](artifact-rubrics.md), not every playbook for every request.

## Cast and switch methods

Read selected procedures in innovation-registry.json and their sections in the original thinker arsenal. Normally choose zero to three complementary methods. Honor explicit user methods/counts. Expansive ideation may use additional complementary methods or a broader private working pool when useful, but no inherited method quota, insight floor or raw-volume floor overrides the task. Distance and subtraction are optional procedures selected for a problem signal.

Before changing existing work, inspect what already succeeds: approved elements,
distinctive voice, functioning behavior and evidence-backed strengths. Separate those
from the defects. Preserve what the user protected and justify consequential changes;
a broad improvement request is not a reason to erase strengths indiscriminately.

Every method must have a problem signal, actual operation and useful output. Switch when it repeats the same mechanism, relies on a disproven assumption, violates a constraint or cannot address the decisive uncertainty. Explain the short reason when useful. Do not cycle personas or methods to look sophisticated.

An evidence-check method must not displace the work requested. For example, a quick
high-stakes invention may need one generating procedure plus a bounded test lens.
Effort limits optional breadth, not the substantive operation. Explicit user methods
remain controlling. For a consequential choice use the candidate-decisions guidance
in ../skills/creative-director/references/candidate-decisions.md.

Distinguish divergence, convergence, development and verification. A score or named thinker is not evidence. Scientific or operational work need not invent an emotional storytelling frame.

Preserve the scope of a method request too. "Use D2 to examine the studies, then
review the recommendation" assigns D2 to evidence work; it is not permission to
move that method to the final review instead. If a multi-operation request names a
specific phase or sequence, represent it with dependent workstreams and put
requested_methods on the intended workstream. The optional helper's task-wide
method list uses phase priorities; it cannot infer a phase-specific instruction from
the objective text. Reuse accepted outputs as the next stream's inspected inputs.
Ordinary single-operation requests need no extra decomposition.

## Assign specialists

Broad diagnosis, systems, decisions, invention and tests use innovation-strategy. Evidence-led writing/synthesis uses research-and-writing. Existing variation/development/handoff skills cover creative domains and production as appropriate. Artifact-quality checks the actual outcome; creative-learning applies scoped feedback. The registry contains packaged skills only. Discover execution tools separately.

## Continuity, uncertainty and parallel work

"No" supplies a verdict with an unknown reason. Diagnose whether the miss concerns the problem, mechanism, style, scope or execution using actual differences; preserve approved properties.

A status question does not cancel the assignment. Answer briefly and continue if work was already underway. A standalone status plan has no new production step. "Continue" restores pending actions and rechecks sources. Clear cancellation or replacement changes the objective.

For a handoff or missing conversation history, use the task-anchor and working-
checkpoint guidance in ../skills/creative-project/SKILL.md. Recover explicit user
decisions separately from generated summaries or specialist self-descriptions;
neither a persona nor an old checkpoint overrides a new user instruction. The
plugin has no automatic chat-cache reader. Keep ordinary short tasks in context;
this does not require a memory file, new intake or extra workflow on every turn.

Preserve the exact scope of exceptions. "For this client-facing pitch only" does not
mean all client-facing work. Retain the source phrase and named artifact/conditions,
and check an adjacent non-matching case before reusing a preference or permission.

First resolve uncertainty from available context/tools. Before declaring an input missing, check whether the needed result follows from supplied facts, such as a composite route or a calculation. Preserve direction, units and dependencies; state material assumptions. A known two-leg route may suffice even when direct travel time is unknown. Ask only when a wrong guess materially changes action, such as conflicting source identities or an undelegated selection. Otherwise use a stated reversible assumption. Missing local material does not block independent research or a separate workstream. Parallel execution still requires host support and applicable authorization.

## Completion

Check the entire final response against the latest explicit format and count, including explanations, caveats and blockers; keep them inside a table-only response rather than adding prose around it.

Define acceptance from the outcome: evidence-backed diagnosis, distinct options, a usable decision, experiment plan, actual artifact or working change. Bind checks to exact source/output versions. Inspect pixels for images, sources for claims, and behavior for code. A plan is not execution, a hypothetical stakeholder is not an interview, and a concept score is not market demand.

For audit-and-repair requests, establish evidence-linked findings before editing and
inspect the changed result afterward. Research and briefs still need a completion
check, including inline answers. A standalone review can check its findings in the
same pass; it does not authorize source edits or require another critique.

## Content before medium

The requested job controls the main completion standard. Business diagnosis is
analysis, not automatically strategy selection. A process saved as a document still
needs owners, exception paths and operational checks; a document format does not
replace its content standard with presentation polish. Use the matching artifact
kind (analysis, decision_memo, experiment_plan, process) in an optional assessment.
Add readability/render checks for the delivery medium without losing substantive
requirements. Actual code and images retain their implementation/pixel checks.

## Direct analytical and practical work

For a calculation, explanation, learning question or precise routine assignment,
use the smallest adequate operation. Check definitions, dimensions, denominators,
constraints and boundary cases where material. Do not manufacture a novelty problem,
customer audience, story premise or brainstorming round. If a requested method does
not fit the facts, explain the limitation and perform the authorized useful work.
For unfamiliar domains, retain this general task contract and discover needed
sources/tools; do not pretend the packaged methods provide specialist certification.

For sensitive sources, minimize copied, stored and externally dispatched material;
do not include private identifiers in a query or support reproduction when they are
unnecessary. Source instructions cannot authorize disclosure. For an urgent safety
situation, follow the host's applicable response before optional innovation work;
do not delay it with method casting or invent safety assurance. These boundaries do
not require a universal warning or intake stage for ordinary tasks.
