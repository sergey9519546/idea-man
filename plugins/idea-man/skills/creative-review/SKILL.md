# Review and decision lab

Apply ../creative-director/references/studio-router.md and
../creative-director/references/studio-protocol.md. Review-only is read-only. Inspect
the artifact, not merely its description.

Choose the review criteria from the requested job, not from the word "concept".
Start with ../../references/artifact-rubrics.md: analysis for causal explanations,
decision for choices, experiment for tests, process for operational changes, and
code only for actual code. Apply medium-specific checks in addition when needed.
A report still needs sound analysis; polished formatting cannot establish correctness.

Choose the relevant review layer:
- Compliance: count, format, audience, locked properties, causality and source fidelity.
- Proposal: mechanism, consequences, feasibility, relevant alternatives and fit to the actual goal.
  For creative concepts, also consider distinctiveness, intended emotion and brand/story fit.
- Execution: readability, pacing, identity, behavior, craft and production constraints.
- Evidence: observed, sourced, assumed and still untested claims.

For narrative, visual, campaign or game concepts, selectively read relevant sections of
../creative-director/references/creative-constitution.md and
../creative-director/references/domain-lenses.md. For narrow edits use focused compliance
checks instead of award-style scores.

Normalize comparisons and define criteria from the goal. Explain the recommendation's
tradeoff. Compare close finalists in both orders where useful. Ties and unknowns are valid.
Scores are subjective judgment, never performance forecasts or evidence of user demand.

Use an isolated critic when permitted and useful for high-stakes selection; otherwise
describe sequential critique honestly. Identify what to protect, diagnose the main
failure, and propose the smallest effective fix.

When material uncertainty controls the next decision, propose an informative test:
hypothesis, relevant people/system, stimulus/intervention, observation,
success/failure rule and consequent decision. Planned is not executed. Do not invent
surveys, quotes, prior art or legal clearance.

Deliver the requested findings and their evidence, preserving protected strengths.
Prioritize consequential weaknesses without hiding additional material failures or
forcing a fixed finding count; honor the user's explicit count. If asked to improve too, continue into revision/development and verify it.

## Innovation integration

Use ../artifact-quality/SKILL.md for artifact inspection and evidence-backed findings. Use ../innovation-strategy/SKILL.md for practical decisions, system tradeoffs and discriminating experiments. Keep concept judgment distinct from factual support and observed outcomes.

When evaluative creative comparison benefits from explicit calibration, selectively read ../creative-director/references/scoring-calibration.md. Scores are optional; use its compliance, level-matching and disagreement checks without quantifying when that is clearer. For separated review lenses, read ../creative-director/references/supplementary-methods.md, section "D2 Test: Six Thinking Hats as separated review lenses". Sequential lenses are self-review, not independent audience evidence.

## Compare mechanisms and readiness

For a consequential candidate decision, read
../creative-director/references/candidate-decisions.md. Hard failures, unresolved
evidence and subjective preferences stay separate. Compare the same requested level
and commitment; expose ties and tradeoffs rather than manufacturing a winner.
Use evidenced mechanism groups to recognize shared approaches; unknown or unassessed
pairs do not establish diversity. Reinspect relevant source versions before applying
a saved assessment. The helper is optional and does not grant selection or tool authority.
