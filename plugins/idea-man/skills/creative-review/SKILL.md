# Review and decision lab

Apply ../creative-director/references/studio-router.md and
../creative-director/references/studio-protocol.md. Review-only is read-only. Inspect
the artifact, not merely its description.

Choose the relevant review layer:
- Compliance: count, format, audience, locked properties, causality and source fidelity.
- Concept: mechanism, distinctiveness, specific emotion, consequence, nearest known
  analogue, and whether this project/brand is essential to the idea.
- Execution: readability, pacing, identity, behavior, craft and production constraints.
- Evidence: observed, sourced, assumed and still untested claims.

For new concepts read relevant sections of
../creative-director/references/creative-constitution.md and
../creative-director/references/domain-lenses.md. For narrow edits use focused compliance
checks instead of award-style scores.

Normalize comparisons and define criteria from the goal. Explain the recommendation's
tradeoff. Compare close finalists in both orders where useful. Ties and unknowns are valid.
Scores are subjective judgment, never performance forecasts or evidence of user demand.

Use an isolated critic when permitted and useful for high-stakes selection; otherwise
describe sequential critique honestly. Identify what to protect, diagnose the main
failure, and propose the smallest effective fix.

Design the cheapest informative test: hypothesis, audience/sample, stimulus, behavior,
success/failure rule and consequent decision. Planned is not executed. Do not invent
surveys, quotes, prior art or legal clearance.

Deliver verdict, protected strength, one to three consequential weaknesses and next
decision. If asked to improve too, continue into revision/development and verify it.

## Innovation integration

Use ../artifact-quality/SKILL.md for artifact inspection and evidence-backed findings. Use ../innovation-strategy/SKILL.md for practical decisions, system tradeoffs and discriminating experiments. Keep concept judgment distinct from factual support and observed outcomes.
