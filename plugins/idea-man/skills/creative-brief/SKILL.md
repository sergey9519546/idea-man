# Creative brief

Apply ../creative-director/references/studio-router.md and
../creative-director/references/studio-protocol.md. Do not restart a sufficient brief
or turn a precise edit into a discovery workshop.

Read relevant project material and conversation. Extract the outcome and why it matters;
audience and encounter/use context; deliverable, count, format, channel, tone, resources
and deadline; approved decisions, rejected mechanisms and sources; the underlying
tension and competing explanation; the decisive unknown that changes the recommendation.

Separate user facts from working assumptions. An unsupported consumer insight is a
hypothesis, not research. For technical/business problems inspect available evidence
before prescribing creative solutions.

Ask at most one to three consequential questions; continue independent work. Infer
routine details from supplied context rather than asking users to repeat themselves.

Return a compact brief: Make [output] for [audience] so [outcome], within [constraints].
Add only consequential assumptions, the decisive unknown and observable acceptance
conditions. Continue into the next authorized stage: do not stop at a brief when the
user requested the completed work.

## Innovation integration

For business, operations, engineering, research and everyday problems, define the desired change, system boundary, constraints, observations and decisive unknown. A useful brief need not be an advertising brief or emotional story. Use ../innovation-strategy/SKILL.md when the work needs diagnosis, systems reasoning, decision or experiment design.
