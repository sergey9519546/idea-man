02 / How it works

INSIGHT FIRST.
THEN IDEAS.

Most brainstorming starts generating before it understands the problem. Idea Man does it in the other order.

  1. 01

    Diagnose the problem.

    What are you really trying to solve, for whom, with what constraints? The brief gets sharpened before any idea appears.

  2. 02

    Find the insight.

    The true thing about people or the situation that makes a good idea inevitable. Everything else is built on it.

  3. 03

    Generate far past the obvious.

    Structural methods drawn from real master thinkers (lateral, systems, divergent and more) push past the first ten ideas everyone has.

  4. 04

    Let a blind critic kill the weak ones.

    A separate critic scores each idea on a calibrated scale. Only 7+ gets presented, with its risks and the cheapest test.

04 / Sample output

WHAT YOU ACTUALLY GET.

Brief
A tiny coffee roaster is launching decaf with $5k.
Insight
People apologise when they order decaf.
The idea
No Reason Needed. Ordering decaf becomes a small ritual, not a confession: an opt-in bell customers can choose to ring, so nobody is singled out.
Why it works
It removes the apology instead of hiding the product.
Killed
  • Rename it “Midnight Roast.” Cosmetic: it hides the label, not the apology.
  • Free decaf Fridays. A discount signals “lesser product,” confirming the stigma.
Cheapest test
One weekend, one counter, one bell and a small sign. Watch whether people order decaf without apologising.

Illustrative example, written to show the format. Your results depend on your brief.
