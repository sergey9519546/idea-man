Idea-Man

Every big idea needs a sidekick.

01 / Logo

The glasses are the logo.

The round cyan glasses are the identity cue and the only small-size mark. The characters are for large placements.

Idea-Man.

Lockup, light. Mark on the left, wordmark in deep violet. Mark height is 1.5x the cap height.

Idea-Man.

Lockup, dark. Same artwork on deep violet. Wordmark in paper white.

App icon. Mark on a solar yellow tile. Ships as icon.png (512 px) and icon-1024.png.

Clear space

Keep a margin equal to one rim diameter (the width of one lens ring, about 42% of the mark width) on every side.

1 rim1 rim

Minimum sizes

Mark: 16 px wide. Lockup: 120 px wide. Below that, use the icon tile or the mark alone.

Don’t

No faces on the bulb. No eyes, brows or mouth.

No filament. No rays or sparks either.

Don’t recolour the glasses. Cyan and violet only.

Don’t stretch or squash. Scale proportionally.

No busy backgrounds. Use flat yellow, paper or deep violet.

02 / Colour

Yellow sets the stage.

Select a swatch to copy its hex. CMYK values are approximate screen-to-print conversions; proof on your press before production.

Contrast ratios

WCAG 2.1: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text (24 px, or 19 px bold) and icons.

Type

Display: Arial Black, then Segoe UI Black, Helvetica Neue, Inter. Normal tracking (about +0.01em) so letters never touch; never tighten the wordmark. Use the exact spelling Idea-Man in the wordmark and prose.

Body: Segoe UI, then the system sans-serif. No external font requests.

03 / Characters

Two ways to show up.

Both are approved. Use them large, never as the small icon. The empty glasses of Invisible Nerd make room for the person with the idea; Lightbulb Hero gives the idea presence.
